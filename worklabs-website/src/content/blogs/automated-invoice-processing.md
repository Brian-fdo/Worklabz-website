### **Section 1: Understanding Automated Invoice Processing**

Automated invoice processing leverages technologies like OCR, ML, and NLP to streamline the often complex accounts payable functions. But what exactly does this entail?

**Core Components:**

1. **OCR (Optical Character Recognition)**: Converts scanned invoices into machine-readable data.
2. **ML (Machine Learning)**: Enhances data extraction by learning from patterns.
3. **NLP (Natural Language Processing)**: Deals with different formats and languages in global transactions.

Using these techniques, invoices move seamlessly from receipt to capture, extraction, and finally to approval, requiring little to no human intervention. Learn more from [Precoro's Automation Guide](https://precoro.com/blog/ai-invoice-processing-automation).
